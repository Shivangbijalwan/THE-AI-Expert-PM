What is a prompt?
Clear instruction + context + goal is a prompt 

Prompt structuring - It is the process where prompting is done and tells a path how it should be done and tells how it should be .

The pathway of a prompt should be - 

Role        -> Who you are 
Context     -> Why you have to do this 
Task        -> What you have to do
Constraints -> How you have to do 


Types of prompting:

1⟫ ROLE PROMPTING ->

Role prompting is the process of giving AI an Identity behaviour thought and role

for example- 

You are a senior AI product manager tell me what a product is ?

here: "You are a senior AI product manager" is a role given to LLM

2⟫ CHAIN OF THOUGHTS ->

prompting in a format of chain is chain prompting

giving a prompt to get the data in steps so its clear how to do work is chain prompting 

for example-

• step by step method
• point wise method 
• give in a proper format 
• analyse and give in points
