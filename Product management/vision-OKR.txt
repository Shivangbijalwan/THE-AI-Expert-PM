1. The Vision
Vision means what we want to achieve in the future.
It focuses on solving a real-life problem.
The solution should be different, useful, and unique from existing solutions.
The vision should aim for a solution where the main problem is completely solved and fewer problems remain.



2. The Strategy
Strategy means how we will achieve our vision.
It identifies the key areas where we need to work.
It helps us make a proper plan to achieve success.
A good strategy makes the goal clear, practical, and achievable.



3. The Roadmap
A roadmap is the step-by-step path to achieve something.
It explains what needs to be done to complete the vision.
It breaks a big goal into smaller and manageable steps.
It provides a clear pattern or direction to follow.



4. The OKRs
OKR = Objectives and Key Results
OKRs define what goals need to be achieved.
They help align the team's objectives with the overall business goals.
OKRs clarify what the team wants to achieve and how success will be measured.
They help the team review progress and make adjustments when needed.
OKRs are mainly about focus and priorities, not just daily tasks.
They help set important priorities for the team.
They also provide a timeline for achieving the desired results.
